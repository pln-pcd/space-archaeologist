## Where the spec in the prompt was checked and adjusted

- **Shadow softening:** the reference multiplies the raw shadow term; we keep the prompt's `smoothstep(0.1, 0.9)` because our PCF soft shadows are already blurred (same look, softer edge).
- **Fog distances:** the reference uses 40 → 300 units for a town; our base is 24 units across and Mars ~150, so the prompt's per-world ranges are right.
- **Camera offset:** confirmed `(0, 1, −5.75)` for a ~1.2-unit character → **(0, 1.4, −8)** for our 1.7-unit explorer; the low pitch is kept inside the base hull clamp (the camera slides in near walls).
- **Breathing shake:** confirmed (0.08, 0.08, 0.02) rad at speed 0.2, multiplied by a sine-noise that rarely reaches 1, so the real motion is small.
- **Text greys:** `#8d8981` / `#989389` measure ≈ 3.2:1 and 2.9:1 on `#f9f2e4`; essential text uses `#5d5a57` / `#6b665e` (prompt values), the light greys only for decoration.

## Deliberately not adopted

- **Multiplayer / P2P:** kids' privacy and offline play.
- **Any reference file:** textures (ramps, LUT, clouds, masks, transition-intro), `.bin` geometry, GLSL, TS, audio, fonts. All ours, generated.
- **Haze, cloud shadows, wind and sway on the Moon:** no atmosphere (accuracy rule). The Moon gets a black sky, crisp dark shadows and ballistic dust only.
- **32 px buttons and light-grey body text:** kid accessibility; same look at ≥ 64 px visible / ≥ 96 px hit area and ≥ 4.5:1 contrast.
- **Hold-drag-to-walk replacing drag-to-orbit:** the player asked for a free drag camera; hold (≥ 300 ms without moving) then drag walks, a quick drag still orbits.
- **Base maintenance drones:** the player asked for nothing in the base to move without a reason; we keep the base calm (gentle "sometimes" sway on plants and the antenna only).
